Ketchikan parcel studio

Who owns the land

Drop the .geojson from the extension. The map draws every parcel, and this browser keeps the file for the next search. The spreadsheet has the same owners and no outlines.

How to get the file

Unzip the extension. In Chrome or Edge, open the extensions page, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. On the borough map, click Owners and mailing. Drop the GeoJSON here.

  • See the share The largest owners take a color. Acres, assessed value, and the share of the file sit in one list.
  • Compare a parcel Comps are ranked by size, value, zoning, subdivision, and distance. A sale price stays with the parcel.
  • Keep the outreach Mark who to call, write a note, and export one mailing address for every owner you flagged.
